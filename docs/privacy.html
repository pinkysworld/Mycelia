Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 21, 2025

Overview

Mycelia: The Underground Network ("Mycelia", "the App") is a premium iOS game. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. Mycelia is a premium, offline game with no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no in-app purchases. The App does not transmit any data from your device to external servers.

Local Data

All game data — including your progress, settings, and sandbox configurations — is stored locally on your device. This data is never transmitted off your device by the App.

Game Center

Mycelia integrates with Apple Game Center for achievements and leaderboards. Game Center is managed entirely by Apple and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. The App itself does not access or store your Game Center personal information beyond what Apple provides through the standard Game Center framework.

Third-Party Services

Mycelia does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no SDKs that collect or share user data.

Children's Privacy

Since the App does not collect any personal data, it is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any user data with third parties. There is no data to share.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Since the App collects no data, significant changes are unlikely.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at mip@gmx.biz.
