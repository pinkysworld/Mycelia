Frequently Asked Questions

Is Mycelia free?

Mycelia is a premium game with a one-time purchase. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no subscriptions.

Does Mycelia require an internet connection?

No. Mycelia is fully playable offline. An internet connection is only needed for Game Center achievements and leaderboards.

My progress was lost. Can I recover it?

Game progress is saved locally on your device. If you have iCloud backups enabled, restoring from a backup may recover your progress. Unfortunately, we cannot recover lost save data remotely.

What devices are supported?

Mycelia requires iOS 17.0 or later and runs on iPhone and iPad.
